# B2B Ad Notes

LinkedIn Ads puts four formats and job-title targeting in front of a billion professionals. On a first campaign, the setup order matters more than the creative.

**Read the full page:** https://linkedin-ads.github.io/

This platform earns its place when the person you need to reach is defined by what they do for work rather than what they browse, because job title, function and industry are first-class targeting inputs here and guesses everywhere else. It is a poor fit for consumer products with broad audiences, and a poor fit for anyone who wants to launch this afternoon, since a company Page has to exist before a campaign can run. The caveat: LinkedIn keeps pricing on a separate page and quotes no figures in its own overview, so treat any rate you read secondhand as unverified.
